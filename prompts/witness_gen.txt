Create {num_witnesses} witnesses as a JSON array. Each witness object must have:

- occupation: job or role (string)

- alibi_claim: what they claim their alibi is (string)
- secret: what they are actually hiding (string)
- prolog_facts: array of valid Prolog fact strings about their location and knowledge

For prolog_facts, use predicates like:
  location_at(name_atom, time_atom, place_atom).
  has_motive(name_atom).
  has_opportunity(name_atom).
  has_alibi(name_atom).

IMPORTANT: All Prolog atoms must be lowercase_with_underscores. No spaces in atom names.

Respond ONLY with a valid JSON array.
